MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 15, 2024,
by and between Acme Consulting Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and
Bright Horizons LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Client").

Section 1. Definitions
"Services" means the consulting and software development services described in
each Statement of Work executed under this Agreement. "Confidential Information"
means any non-public information disclosed by either party, whether marked
confidential or not, including business plans, technical data, and pricing.

Section 2. Term and Termination
2.1 This Agreement commences on March 15, 2024 and continues for an initial term
of twenty-four (24) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this
Section 2.
2.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90)
days' prior written notice to the other party.
2.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if
the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach
within thirty (30) days of receiving notice of the breach.
2.4 Upon termination, Client shall pay Provider for all Services performed up to
the effective date of termination, including any non-cancellable expenses.

Section 3. Fees and Payment
3.1 Client shall pay Provider the fees set forth in each Statement of Work.
3.2 Provider shall invoice Client monthly in arrears. All invoices are due within
thirty (30) days of the invoice date.
3.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month, or the
maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.
3.4 The total value of this Agreement, inclusive of all Statements of Work
executed as of the Effective Date, is estimated at $450,000.

Section 4. Limitation of Liability
4.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO
THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO PROVIDER IN THE TWELVE
(12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.
4.2 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL,
CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, EVEN IF ADVISED OF
THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.
4.3 The limitations in this Section 4 shall not apply to breaches of Section 5
(Confidentiality) or claims arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct.

Section 5. Confidentiality
5.1 Each party agrees to hold the other party's Confidential Information in
strict confidence and not to disclose it to any third party without prior
written consent, except as required by law.
5.2 This obligation of confidentiality shall survive termination of this
Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

Section 6. Indemnification
6.1 Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from any
third-party claims arising from Provider's gross negligence or willful
misconduct in performing the Services.
6.2 Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider from any
third-party claims arising from Client's misuse of the deliverables.

Section 7. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware,
without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
7.2 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be submitted to
good-faith negotiation between senior executives of both parties. If unresolved
within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be settled by binding arbitration in
Wilmington, Delaware under the rules of the American Arbitration Association.

Section 8. Intellectual Property
8.1 All pre-existing intellectual property of Provider used in delivering the
Services remains the sole property of Provider.
8.2 Upon full payment, Client shall own all deliverables specifically created
for Client under a Statement of Work, excluding any Provider pre-existing IP
incorporated therein, which is licensed to Client on a perpetual, royalty-free
basis.

Section 9. Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance due to
circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, acts
of government, labor disputes, or failures of telecommunications infrastructure.

Section 10. Miscellaneous
10.1 This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work, constitutes the
entire agreement between the parties.
10.2 This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both
parties.
10.3 Notices under this Agreement shall be sent to the addresses set forth in
the signature block below.
